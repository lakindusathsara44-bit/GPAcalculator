How priorities work

Priority 1 · Critical

Foundational or important syllabus knowledge that may need extra explanation, practice or a stronger reference.

Priority 2 · High

Practical skills, industry tools, modern technologies and employability-related learning.

Priority 3 · Supporting

Advanced references, research and optional enrichment beyond the core learning path.

Priority is based on educational value and relevance, not simply on whether a topic is absent from SLIATE LMS. This first release does not claim to have audited every LMS module for missing content. Discovery links open search results; they are not individually verified resources.

Open LMS is an independent supplementary student resource directory. It is not an official SLIATE service. External content is hosted by third parties and may change.
